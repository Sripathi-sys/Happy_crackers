About Happy Enjoy Crackers

Our Story

For over two decades, Happy Enjoy Crackers has been the trusted name in premium fireworks and celebrations across Tamil Nadu. What started as a small family business in Sivakasi has grown into one of the most respected fireworks retailers in the region.

Founded in 2000 by Mr. Raj Kumar, our journey began with a simple mission: to bring joy and excitement to every celebration with safe, high-quality fireworks. Today, we continue this legacy with the same passion and commitment to excellence.

Our extensive collection includes everything from traditional sparklers and ground chakkars to spectacular aerial displays, all sourced from certified manufacturers and tested for safety and quality.
